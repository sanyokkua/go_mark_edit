## Root causes (evidence-grounded)

1. **Process was rebuilt faster than the product** — 10 regimes in 7 weeks; process configs +46.8k lines vs product +39.9k; `AGENTS.md` ×15 rewrites vs constitution ×0 amendments (`b9d8a05`).
2. **Every regime change deleted the previous instrument without a replacement**: traceability (`927d15f`), agents (`3af7c58`), validators (`53af8e4` — inside a `feat` commit), then re-invented (`cb48e68`).
3. **Contracts committed before measurement**: the zero-pixel parity target (`7744cc8`) scored 0/1,620 on first run and burned W33 (196 commits, 70 fixes, 66 paperwork) before withdrawal (`23ba4b5`).
4. **Evidence displaced engineering**: 189.6k lines of evidence vs 39.9k product; `tasks.md` touched in 52% of commits and used as a diary (2,475 lines, 910 lines >300 chars).
5. **Planning collapsed**: 39 planned → 196 tasks (80% appended), 13 "convergence phases" appended inside `tasks.md`; the roadmap frozen at "Phase 02 in progress".
6. **No review checkpoint**: 29 feature commits in 6 h on Aug 8, 41 commit-pairs <5 min apart Aug 13–17, zero merges, the prescribed branch protocol never executed, the one squash (`f1fa1916`) discarded.
7. **Authority forked**: `AGENTS.md:10` vs `constitution.md:24,169` vs stale `CLAUDE.md` on `master` (still R1); ADR-0028 contradicted by `main.go:166` with no superseding record; decisions scattered into `spec.md` markers, `decisions-phase-21.md`, and memory notes.
8. **Test doubles became the target**: a 2,024-line hand-written mock backend and Playwright wired to it (`KNOWN_ISSUES` #3) mean the e2e suite proves the mock, not the app — which is why "195/196 done" coexisted with owner-discovered defects.
