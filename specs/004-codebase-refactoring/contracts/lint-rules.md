is the single owner of every mechanical rule; the prose instruction for a rule is removed once its
check works. No rule may be suppressed or allowlisted to make a stage pass.

## Forbidden assertion kinds → named deletions → recurrence guard

The constitution (VII) and FR-029 forbid five assertion kinds and any test-only branch in
production. The spec's edge case forbids porting such an assertion into a linter, so each kind is
owned by the named deletions in plan.md's per-suite tables; the guard column names what stops the
kind from returning.
