## What this establishes, and what it does not

**Establishes:** the 2026-08-13 conclusion that the Feature 002 editor term is
"by far the largest single lever remaining on this matrix" is **too narrow**. It
accounts for 432 of 1,638. A further 918 comparisons carry pixel drift of their
own, 621 of them above 100,000 pixels, and those are Feature 003's surfaces —
prompts, launchers, conflicts, previews. Resolving the Feature 002 term would
leave the matrix red.

**Does not establish** what causes the 918. A modal-bearing state whose reference
shows a base screen differs from production across the whole region, and that
reads identically in this report to a genuine styling difference. The prompt,
launcher and conflict states dominating the table above are exactly the states
where that confusion is most likely, and they are also the states no targeted
slice ever converged: T058–T064 covered the menubar, File, Settings, View, About,
tabs, toolbar, editor/status and paused preview, all of which are the converged
end of the manifest. Distinguishing "unpaired state" from "unconverged surface"
for those seven state families is the next real measurement, and it should be
taken before any estimate is put on closing them.
